Live Music for Restaurants & Wineries

Piano and acoustic guitar tailored to your venue's atmosphere.

Venues & Settings

Live music works beautifully in a variety of restaurant and winery settings:

  • Dining Rooms – Professional ambiance during service hours
  • Patios & Outdoor Spaces – Warm atmosphere for al fresco dining
  • Tasting Rooms – Elegant backdrop for wine events and tastings
  • Wine Bars & Cocktail Lounges – Background music or featured performance
  • Cafes & Coffee Shops – Acoustic guitar for daytime or evening ambiance
  • Recurring Music Nights – Monthly, weekly, or seasonal performances

Volume & Atmosphere

The key to successful restaurant and winery music is balance. Devin is experienced in:

Controlled Volume

Sound is carefully managed so music enhances rather than overpowers conversation and dining.

Reading the Room

Performance adjusts to the energy and pacing of your space throughout the evening.

Relaxed Background Music

Sophisticated, familiar repertoire that creates ambiance without demanding attention.

Interactive Performance

When appropriate, can build engagement with sing-alongs and audience-friendly moments.
