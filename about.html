About Devin Frank

Piano and acoustic guitar performances shaped by thoughtful preparation, professional reliability, and an easygoing presence.

The Approach

Devin brings an adaptable mix of piano, acoustic guitar, and vocals to restaurants, wineries, weddings, churches, private events, and community gatherings. Each performance is shaped around the venue, the audience, and the mood of the room.

Rather than fitting a single style or energy, performances flex between relaxed background music and more interactive, audience-friendly moments—whatever serves the event best.

The foundation is strong: familiar songs that resonate across generations, professional preparation, careful attention to volume and pacing, and genuine connection to the people in the room.

Piano & Acoustic Guitar

Devin performs on two instruments, but never simultaneously during a song. Each brings its own character and repertoire:

Piano

Rich, full sound ideal for formal settings and intimate venues. Strong repertoire includes Billy Joel, Elton John, Coldplay, classic standards, and contemporary favorites. Perfect for elegant dinners, ceremonies, and sophisticated atmospheres.

Acoustic Guitar

Warm, personal sound that works for casual and elegant settings alike. Versatile repertoire spans folk, country, rock, and pop—from campfire classics to current hits. Great for outdoor events, relaxed venues, and diverse audiences.

What to Expect

Professional Sound

High-quality audio equipment designed for intimate venues. Sound is polished and professional while remaining warm and approachable.

Compact Setup

The physical footprint is minimal—just one person, one instrument, and professional equipment. Flexible enough to fit most spaces.

Volume Matched to Your Room

Experienced in adjusting performance level and volume to support conversation, dining, dancing, or focused listening—whatever your event needs.

Reliability & Preparation

Thorough preparation, professional punctuality, and a focus on the details. You can trust that the logistics will be handled smoothly.

Familiar Repertoire

Strong foundation of songs that work across generations and moods. Can discuss custom arrangements or special requests during planning.

Reading the Room

Thoughtful about pacing, energy, and engagement. Performances flow naturally, shaped in real time by the space and audience.

The Music

The song selection spans decades and genres—classics and contemporary favorites that resonate. Whether it's a packed restaurant, an intimate dinner, a wedding ceremony, or a community festival, the core is built on songs people know, enjoy, and connect to.

Sets can move smoothly from relaxed background music into audience-friendly sing-alongs, or stay focused and refined—depending on the event's tone and energy.
