About Devin Frank

Working musician. Gear enthusiast. Writing about live performance, music technology, and the business of being a solo artist.

This site documents two parts of the same work: playing music in real rooms and learning what works through actual experience.

Working Musician

Devin performs on piano and acoustic guitar for restaurants, wineries, weddings, churches, festivals, private events, and community gatherings across Northwest Ohio and nearby Southeast Michigan.

Each performance is adapted to the venue, audience, and event. Rather than a single style, performances flex between relaxed background music and more interactive moments—whatever serves the room.

Music Technology & Gear

Beyond performing, Devin is deeply engaged with the tools that make live music possible: keyboards, mixing, sound reinforcement, backing track systems, and workflow optimization.

Rather than collecting gear for its own sake, the focus is on practical, portable, reliable equipment that serves real gigs in actual venues.

This hands-on experience feeds directly into the Field Notes and Gear directory—real-world documentation instead of specifications or marketing copy.

Content & Writing

Field Notes is a collection of articles about what a working musician actually encounters: reading a room, choosing equipment, managing volume in different venues, building a portable rig, and navigating the practical side of solo performance.

This isn't theoretical. It comes from dozens of real gigs, experiments with gear, and honest reflection on what works and what doesn't.

The Approach

Everything here is built on honest experience and practical testing, not marketing or speculation.

Don't invent expertise.

If something hasn't been tested or lived, we say so.

Document real use.

Observations come from actual gigs, real venues, and actual performances.

Prioritize clarity.

Practical information over jargon. Actionable over impressive-sounding.

Respect your time.

Real content, not filler. Articles are substantial or they aren't published.

Stay independent.

No sponsored content without clear labeling. Affiliate links only when they add value.

Piano & Acoustic Guitar

Devin performs on two instruments, but never simultaneously during a song. Each brings its own character and repertoire:

Piano

Rich, full sound ideal for formal settings and intimate venues. Strong repertoire includes Billy Joel, Elton John, Coldplay, classic standards, and contemporary favorites. Perfect for elegant dinners, ceremonies, and sophisticated atmospheres.

Acoustic Guitar

Warm, personal sound that works for casual and elegant settings alike. Versatile repertoire spans folk, country, rock, and pop—from campfire classics to current hits. Great for outdoor events, relaxed venues, and diverse audiences.

What to Expect

Professional Sound

High-quality audio equipment designed for intimate venues. Sound is polished and professional while remaining warm and approachable.

Compact Setup

The physical footprint is minimal—just one person, one instrument, and professional equipment. Flexible enough to fit most spaces.

Volume Matched to Your Room

Experienced in adjusting performance level and volume to support conversation, dining, dancing, or focused listening—whatever your event needs.

Reliability & Preparation

Thorough preparation, professional punctuality, and a focus on the details. You can trust that the logistics will be handled smoothly.

Familiar Repertoire

Strong foundation of songs that work across generations and moods. Can discuss custom arrangements or special requests during planning.

Reading the Room

Thoughtful about pacing, energy, and engagement. Performances flow naturally, shaped in real time by the space and audience.

The Music

The song selection spans decades and genres—classics and contemporary favorites that resonate. Whether it's a packed restaurant, an intimate dinner, a wedding ceremony, or a community festival, the core is built on songs people know, enjoy, and connect to.

Sets can move smoothly from relaxed background music into audience-friendly sing-alongs, or stay focused and refined—depending on the event's tone and energy.
