Disclosures & Privacy

Transparency about editorial independence, affiliate relationships, and how we respect your privacy.

Editorial Independence

Devin Frank Live is a personal resource created by a working musician. The opinions, observations, and recommendations here are based on real experience and honest assessment—not compensation, sponsorship, or manufacturer relationships.

We don't fake expertise. When you read about gear, performance techniques, or business practices here, they come from actual use and real gigs. If something hasn't been tested or verified, we say so.

Affiliate Links

Some future articles may include affiliate links to products or services mentioned. If that happens:

  • The article will clearly disclose the affiliate relationship
  • Affiliate relationships will not change the price you pay
  • Only products and services actually used or tested will be recommended
  • Affiliate income is secondary to editorial honesty

Currently: No active affiliate partnerships. Any future partnerships will be clearly labeled.

Sponsored Content

If any content is sponsored by a manufacturer, vendor, or partner, it will be clearly labeled as such. Sponsored content will:

  • Always be clearly marked with a "Sponsored" or "Sponsored Content" label
  • Include honest assessment, not just promotion
  • Not compromise editorial independence on other content
  • Be distinguished from regular Field Notes articles

Currently: No sponsored content published. If that changes, it will be transparent.

Loaned or Provided Equipment

If gear or equipment is loaned, provided, or gifted for testing or review, it will be clearly disclosed in the article. Receiving equipment doesn't guarantee a positive review—only honest assessment.

Currently: All gear mentioned here is personally owned or actively used in real performances.

Privacy & Email

If you subscribe to Field Notes updates:

  • Your email address will be stored securely and never sold or shared
  • You'll receive updates about new articles, gear observations, and gig lessons
  • You can unsubscribe anytime with one click
  • We respect your inbox—no spam, no daily emails, just genuine updates

Website Privacy

This website doesn't track you with cookies, pixels, or invasive analytics. We respect your privacy while understanding general visitor patterns.

  • No personal data is collected without your consent
  • Contact forms and newsletter signups are secure
  • We don't sell or share your information

Content Accuracy

We strive for accuracy in all technical information, product details, and performance guidance. If you find an error or outdated information, please let us know so we can correct it.
