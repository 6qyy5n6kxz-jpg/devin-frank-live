Wedding Services

Ceremony Music

Processional, recessional, and meaningful moments during the ceremony. Piano or acoustic guitar, customized to your choices and timeline.

Cocktail Hour

Elegant background music during photos and reception setup. Creates ambiance while guests mingle and enjoy refreshments.

Dinner Music

Sophisticated dinner ambiance during the meal service. Volume and energy calibrated to enhance the evening without overpowering conversation.

Reception Support

Background music during dancing, toasts, and celebrations. Can be interactive when appropriate for the event's energy.

Special Songs & Customization

Special songs may be learned in advance of your wedding. If you have specific pieces—for the processional, a meaningful family favorite, or a personal favorite—discuss these during planning, and Devin will prepare them with care.

Note: Devin is a pianist and guitarist, not a DJ. Reception support means live background music; if you need dancing music and entertainment later in the evening, you may want to coordinate that separately.

Planning Your Wedding Music

Start with a conversation about:

  • Date, time, and venue
  • Ceremony music and preferences
  • Cocktail hour and dinner expectations
  • Any special songs or moments
  • Reception timeline and audio needs
  • Venue logistics and setup considerations

Devin will help you think through the details and make sure everything comes together seamlessly.
