Typical Booking Process

1

Initial Inquiry

Tell Devin about your event: date, location, type, duration, and any special requests or songs you have in mind.

2

Availability & Pricing Discussion

Pricing is based on date, location, duration, production needs, and event type. Devin will provide a quote tailored to your event.

3

Event Planning

Collaborate on details: venue logistics, sound setup, performance flow, special requests, and any unique requirements.

4

Confirmation & Preparation

Once details are finalized, booking is confirmed. Devin will prepare thoroughly to ensure a seamless performance.

5

Performance Day

Devin arrives early for setup and sound check. Professional, reliable service tailored to your event.
