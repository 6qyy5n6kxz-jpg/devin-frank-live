Live Music for Churches

Piano and acoustic guitar for worship services, special music, and faith-centered gatherings.

Services for Churches

Special Music

Featured performances during worship services. Solo piano or guitar pieces that complement your service and message.

Worship Support

Instrumental support during congregational singing, worship times, or reflective moments. Professional, prepared, and rooted in faith.

Church Services

Traditional and contemporary services. Adaptable to your church's style, denomination, and worship approach.

Outreach & Community Events

Community programs, faith-centered gatherings, fundraisers, and special celebrations hosted by your church.

Additional Occasions

Beyond weekly services, live music can serve your church at:

  • Funerals & Memorial Services – Respectful, meaningful music to honor lives and support families
  • Weddings – Ceremony and reception music (see wedding services for details)
  • Holiday Services – Christmas, Easter, and other seasonal celebrations
  • Church Anniversaries – Special milestones and celebrations
  • Fundraising Events – Support for mission work, charitable causes, and community outreach
  • Youth & Children's Events – Age-appropriate, engaging performances

Approach & Preparation

Devin approaches church music with:

Spiritual Respect

Understanding that your music serves a faith community and matters deeply to those present.

Thorough Preparation

Learning your church's traditions, style, and specific needs. Practicing carefully and arriving ready to serve.

Professional Flexibility

Adaptable to different worship styles, from traditional hymns to contemporary songs to instrumental pieces.

Advance Coordination

Early planning and communication to align with your pastor, music director, and service flow.

Repertoire

Strong repertoire includes:

  • Traditional hymns and sacred music
  • Contemporary Christian favorites
  • Gospel and soul standards
  • Instrumental classics
  • Familiar worship songs
  • Meaningful secular selections when appropriate

Custom arrangements or special songs can be learned in advance of your service.

Getting Started

To discuss live music for your church:

  • Reach out to Devin Frank with your date, type of service or event, and music needs
  • Share your church's style, worship approach, and any specific preferences
  • Discuss the logistics: sanctuary setup, sound support, rehearsal availability
  • Plan details well in advance for the best preparation and service

Contact Your Church Music Needs

Whether it's a one-time special service, a recurring role, or a specific occasion, Devin is available to support your church's music and mission.
